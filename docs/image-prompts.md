## Sculpture Égée

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: original panoramic illustration for a Mediterranean editorial website, 3:2 landscape, high resolution. Deep rich Aegean blue sky and Mediterranean sea, warm afternoon sunlight, beautiful carved ivory marble, art-book print texture.
A monumental classical Greek marble statue of an adult male philosopher, fully dressed in a thick sculpted ivory chiton tunic and a draped robe covering the entire body and chest, dominates the RIGHT half. This is an obvious stone sculpture in an open-air museum, not a real person. Curly carved hair, noble thoughtful profile looking left, head and shoulders with finely sculpted folds of the garments. His entire head is visible and starts 15 percent from top. A partial Doric column at far right, a restrained pale gold sun disk behind the head, tiny olive leaves at extreme top right. Low horizon with dark blue sea, a pale Greek island in distance. The LEFT 48 percent is calm uninterrupted deep cobalt blue (#0867a0), reserved for white website typography. Most of the sea remains subtle in the bottom fifth. Sophisticated original classical collage, vintage Mediterranean travel-poster atmosphere combined with photographic marble texture. Delicate analog grain. Golden warm light and clear architectural shadows. Figure cropped at lower torso by bottom edge, fully covered in robes. No nude sculpture, no bare chest, no naked body. No text, no letters, no logo, no border, no interface, no watermark, no sparkles. Create the illustration only.

## Ascension méditerranéenne

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: original cinematic poster illustration for a Mediterranean fitness brand called Aufstieg. Landscape 16:9, no text. A sculptural ancient Greek ivory marble stairway ascending from the bottom left towards an elegant fluted Doric colonnade on the right, on a sun-drenched Greek seaside cliff. Large rich cobalt and Aegean blue sky and sea. A single olive tree with fine natural leaves in a pale stone pot in the left foreground, casting a delicate afternoon shadow on the weathered marble steps. Golden late-afternoon sunlight raking across ivory marble. The ocean and a distant hazy rocky Greek island fill background. Luxury European art-direction with vintage travel-poster composition, tactile film grain, refined warm limestone, rich cobalt blue, olive green. Monumental yet peaceful, exquisitely architectural, big simple shapes and photographed natural texture, not a flat illustration, not glossy CGI. Elegant quiet spaciousness. No people, no text, no branding, no UI, no watermarks.
